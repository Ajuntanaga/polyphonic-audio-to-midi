## 3. The lane error: what it looks like (saved table, `ordinary-current-on-512`)

- **Persistent per note, not frame noise.** Rendered genuine notes at 61–84: 120 runs (same label and note, consecutive ticks), 28 lane switches in 6,753 ticks. Run-weighted: 48 runs entirely on the annotated lane, 67 never, 5 mixed. The 61.5% is tick-weighted and propped up by a few long runs (labels 485: 1,088 ticks 78%; 292: 1,043 ticks 99%; 131: 978 ticks 76%; 68: 877 ticks 0%). Run-level accuracy is about 40%.
- **Directional.** Across all bands, 240,258 rendered pitch-correct voices: 61.6% on the annotated lane, and of the wrong ones 84.1% sit on a HIGHER string index (lower fret) than annotated (<48: 87%, 48–59: 81%, 60–71: 97%; 72–84 reverses, 4%). At 61–84 the commonest confusions are annotated 5→6 (812), 6→7 (396), 4→5 (311), 5→7 (255), 4→7 (242), 3→5 (240).
- **Not a chord-assignment artifact.** With a single voice in the tick the DP is right in 822 of 1,302 (63%); two voices 70%; three or more 49%.
- **Not a string-index mismatch.** Assigned string 5 is on the annotated lane in 24 of 13,582 voices (48–59: 24/12,902; 60–71: 0/680) but those voices are annotated on strings 0–4 (2,927 + 2,556 + 4,307 + 2,782 + 262 at 48–59); assigned string 7 at 60–71 is right 0 of 1,804. Strings 3 and 4 at 60–71 are right 99.5% and 97.0%. The confusion matrices are spread, not a permutation (`...LANE-CONFUSION-ALL-BANDS...json`).
- **The saved per-string evidence does not carry the lane.** Over all 206,131 single-lane genuine frames at 61–84 (any selection state), `best_string` equals the annotated lane 32.0% of the time, rising with string index (lane 3: 8.8%, 4: 15.5%, 5: 29.8%, 6: 41.6%, 7: 60.6%): a fret prior, not string identity. `fine_valid` is 0 in all 518,053 genuine single-lane frames of the table. The source has a 3.0 s minimum evidence window (`kFineFrequencyMinimumEvidenceSeconds`, polyphonic_pitch_detector.cpp:81) and labelled notes here last about 0.75 s (1,122 frames × 64 samples / 96 kHz), so the field cannot become valid on this dataset; I have not checked whether the long/isolated datasets or the installed configuration exercise it. `committed_mask` (which looks like the offline spectral-onset latch in native/offline/unison_spectral_shadow.cpp; I only read the grep, not the logic) is set in 71% of frames below 48, 65% at 48–59, 5.9% at 60–71 and 0% at 72–84, and when set it contains the annotated lane in 67%, 52% and 63%.

## 4. Proposal (equal standing: I recommend changing the priority)

**Mechanism.** The detector chooses a string for each pitch from evidence that does not identify strings, and its choice leans toward lower frets, held for the whole note. Above note 60 five strings play every pitch, so this error is largest exactly where relief wanted to add voices. Pitch recovery and lane identity are separable, and the user's stated goal (each tuner locks to its string) is the lane.

**Step 0 — no new processing, a download only (Codex's call).** The trial's snapshot and raw MIDI are in the remote evidence artifact `m3-evidence-44881108478-1` (GitHub artifact 14698665226, 6.5 MB), which `artifacts.json` says expires 2026-11-28. Collecting it once lets us attribute the +7,810 wrong-string, +3,704 gap and the 13/56 chatter to labels and ticks, and see the lane relieved notes actually received. That resolves my caveats 3 and the per-label disagreement in section 2 without any new run.

**Step 1 — a lane-evidence observation, default-off, trace-only (needs its own authorization).** Add to the candidate trace for notes ≥ 56: the full eight-string per-string option score before and after the fretted penalty, the DP's chosen string, and whether the choice is held from the previous tick. Purpose: find out whether any per-string quantity separates the annotated lane at run level, which the saved fields cannot (section 3). Unit of analysis is the run, not the tick, because errors persist per run. Baselines to beat on this dataset, from my scripts: run-level all-correct 48/120 = 40%, none-correct 67/120 = 56%.

**Step 2 — only if step 1 finds a signal.** A default-off lane rule on the existing selection, scored at run level, with pitch safety and lane gates declared before the screen: wrong-note, gap, retrigger and held-sample gates unchanged (zero growth), plus a lane non-inferiority gate (lane accuracy of the output at or above the CURRENT baseline per band and per assigned string, so a rule that fixes string 5 cannot break strings 3 and 4). Relief is revisited after lane, not before: under the current zero-growth wrong-string gate, any pitch recovery at the DP's lane accuracy fails by construction. I am not asking to relax that gate for the frozen relief; if it is split, the split should be declared in advance for new candidates only.

## 5. Strongest saved counterexamples the lane rule must survive

- **Label 68 vs label 292.** Label 68 (note 64, annotated string 5) spends 877 ticks on strings 4, 6 and 7 and is never right; label 292 (note 66, annotated string 6) spends 1,043 ticks on 5/6 and is 99.2% right. A global shift of the fret preference toward higher frets would fix 68 and could break 292 and the 97–99.5% strings 3–4 at 60–71; the rule must be string- or evidence-specific, and the non-inferiority gate catches the reverse.
- **Labels 13 and 56** (NoteOns 0→12 and 0→11 under relief; 10 and 1 eligible runs in the saved table): any rule that makes a lane persist by hysteresis must not turn fragmented evidence into chatter.
- **Label 42's 469 ms false hold** (703 ticks above the floor, rendered) remains the standing counterexample to anything that keeps notes alive; the relief screen added +238,421 gap samples with +3 onsets, i.e. extension, not new attacks.
